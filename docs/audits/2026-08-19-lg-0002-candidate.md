## Change unit

Problem: LG-0001 accepts already-normalized `SourceSnapshot.authority`, so a caller could assign
authority without provenance binding.

Beneficiary: CASER-SOURCER and future provider adapters.

Expected improvement: provider observations become authority-free inputs; trusted exact-match
bindings assign authority, emit provenance, and fail closed when identity/authority is unresolved.

## Security / privacy boundary

- No private Google Drive IDs are committed.
- No credentials, tokens, cookies, authorization headers, or arbitrary locator keys are accepted
  into provenance.
- No network/provider calls are added to the graph.
- No write/remediation/deployment node is added.
- No production or external-provider mutation is in scope.
